Privacy Policy for Putt Battle

Last updated: [DATE]

Putt Battle ("the app", "we", "us") is an app for disc golf putting and approach practice, built to be played socially together with other players. This policy describes what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Summary

Putt Battle is designed to work without requiring personal data for its core functionality. You can start a match, play with friends, and view results without creating an account or providing your name, email, or other identifying information, unless stated otherwise below.

2. Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, the following information may be handled:

  • Game data (local): Scores, statistics, personal records, and match history stored locally on your device so the app can function (e.g. for Solo Mode and match summaries).
  • Account information (if you choose to create an account): If the app offers login and cloud sync, we may process your email address, an optional display name/profile, and match data linked to your account, in order to save your history across devices.
  • Technical information: Basic technical information such as operating system, device type, and crash reports may be collected automatically by the app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) under their own policies, independently of us.
  • We do not collect: Location/GPS, contact lists, camera/microphone content, or other sensitive information beyond what is explicitly described above.

3. How the information is used

  • To make the app function as intended (display scores, statistics, and match results)
  • To save and sync your history across devices, if you have an account
  • To troubleshoot and improve the app's performance and stability
  • We never sell your information to third parties
  • We do not show ads based on personal data

4. Storage

Game data that does not require an account is stored locally on your device and is not shared with us. If the account feature is used, data is stored with our cloud service providers under industry-standard security measures. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. If the app is integrated with third-party services in the future (e.g. UDisc or smartwatches), this will be described here before the feature is activated, and will require your explicit consent.

6. Children

Putt Battle is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from users in that age group. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

If you have an account with us, you have the right to:

  • Request the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves, for example when new features (accounts, leagues, statistics) are launched. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page, with an updated date at the top.
